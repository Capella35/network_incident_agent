| 2026-11-20 | **Modeled KPI model** (`src/producer/kpi_model.yaml`): load = `traffic_internet`; capacity = per-cell training-week q99 × 1.25 (floor 10% of the median); `latency_ms` = 20 / (1 − min(ρ, 0.98)) × log-normal noise (σ 0.05); `drop_rate` = 0.002 + 0.198 × logistic((ρ − 0.95) / 0.03) × noise (σ 0.1). | 2026-11-22 | **Postgres sink (Step 8):** consumer group `telemetry-sink`, manual offset commits, batches of ≤ 500. Order per batch: Postgres commit → DLQ acknowledged → Kafka offset commit; any failure before the offset commit re-delivers the batch, and the upsert on `(replay_run_id, cell_id, interval_start)` makes that harmless. On a Postgres data error the batch is retried row by row and only failing rows are dead-lettered (no poison pill). The sink re-validates every message with `common.telemetry`; the schema now requires `latency_ms > 0` to match the table's CHECK. Reason: effectively-once delivery without Kafka transactions, and one bad row can never stall the stream. |
